Why this pace works

Planner decisions informed by Indian traveller experiences

Recent India-to-Japan accounts repeatedly emphasise airport setup, jet-lag recovery, IC-card convenience and neighbourhood clustering. This plan applies those lessons without copying anyone’s itinerary.

🧳

Hotel and bags first

Airport → Nippori → reception is the first route. Sightseeing begins only after large luggage is safely stored.

🕰️

Arrival day stays light

Immigration and a 3.5-hour time difference are treated as real travel load. The afternoon contains a long shower-and-rest block.

🗺️

Districts are grouped

West Tokyo, the bay/tower corridor, and Tokyo Station/Asakusa are handled on separate days to avoid repeated cross-city transfers.

🍛

Meals are appointments

Indian lunch and dinner are fixed 60–75 minute seated breaks near the day’s route. Reserve four seats where possible and save one nearby backup before leaving India.
